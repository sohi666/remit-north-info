Remit North

Privacy · Terms · Français

Remit North calculates payroll source deductions for every province and territory (CPP/QPP, EI, QPIP, federal and provincial income tax), GST/HST/QST under the regular or Quick Method, corporate income tax, and the best salary and dividend mix for owner-managers.

Tables follow the CRA T4127 payroll formulas and Revenu Québec publications. The app downloads new tables when they are published and works offline.

Support

A question, a calculation that looks wrong, or an idea? Write to support@cibinc.ca. Include the province, pay frequency and amount, and the result you expected.

Remit North gives estimates. It is not tax, legal or accounting advice, and the app is not affiliated with the Government of Canada, the CRA or Revenu Québec.
