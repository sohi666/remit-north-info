Privacy policy

Remit North · Français

Effective November 14, 2026. This policy explains what information the app Remit North collects. Remit North is published by Consultation Behtoyi Inc. ("we"), La Prairie, Québec, Canada.

In short: no personal data collected

Remit North asks for no account, name, email or phone number. The salaries, sales and other amounts you enter are used only for calculations on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

What the app downloads

To keep tax rates current, the app downloads a public file of tax tables from https://sohi666.github.io/remit-north-info/tables/. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to the hosting provider (GitHub). We receive no information from these requests and send nothing about you or your calculations.

What stays on your device

Your last inputs and settings (province, language, appearance) and the downloaded tables are stored only on your device. They may be included in your device backup, depending on your own settings. Deleting the app erases them.

Ads, purchases, analytics

This version shows no ads, has no in-app purchases, and uses no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. If a future version adds any, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Your rights and who is responsible

For any question about personal information, write to support@cibinc.ca. The person in charge of the protection of personal information is the president of Consultation Behtoyi Inc., reachable at the same address. You may also contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Changes

We will post any change on this page with its effective date.
